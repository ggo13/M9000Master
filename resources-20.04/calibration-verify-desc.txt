Calibration VERIFICATION Report

Summary:   A self-calibration is run to produce Calibration Factors required to
           bring the DFR into calibration. These Calibration Factors are compared
           to the Calibration Factors that have been applied. The purpose is to
           determine whether the applied Calibration Factors are still valid.

           (The Calibration Factors that are produced during the self-calibration
           are not applied to the DFR.)

           There are four possible gains available: x1, x3, x6, & x8. A gain
           scale factor and offset factor are determined and verified for all four gains.
